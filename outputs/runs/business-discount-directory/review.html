PASSING WITH FLAGS 修订 0/3 · 流程步数 5 · 审核材料同步存于 review.md

怎么审（约 5 分钟）

  1. 看硬指标（校验结果）：有没有 ❌。校验位、枚举、唯一性是代码算的，不会说谎。
  2. 看语义疑点（语义抽检）：这是最需要人的部分——模型挑出「解析没错但含义可能错」的字段，你判断同不同意。
  3. 抽看对照样例（原始 vs 输出）：随手挑两条，问自己「左边这列的意思，真的是右边那个字段吗？」
  4. 扫一眼未映射清单（unmapped）：理由是否诚实，有没有本该映射却被偷懒放弃的列。

你只有三个动作：approve / reject + 意见 / abandon。你不能直接改配置文件——人一改，配置就成了「手写的」，作业的核心主张（agent 独立完成映射）就不成立了。

entity.trading_nameTrading Name["trim", "empty_to_null"]0.9Column 'Trading Name' holds values like 'Amplifon', 'Andersens Floor Coverings' and 'Access Dental Services Pty Ltd'. These are the names the businesses trade under in the discount directory, repeated across their outlets. It is not entity.legal_name because it is not labelled as a registered name and no register confirms it. 'Outlet Name' is the nearer outlet-level label and is left unmapped.
entity.websiteWebsite["trim", "empty_to_null", "url_normalize"]0.8Column 'Website' holds values like 'http://www.andersens.com.au', 'http://adpa.com.au' and 'https://andrewsairportpark.com.au'. They are the business's web address. Some are malformed at source, e.g. 'http://https://www.amplifon.com/au/'. Blank for about 8.5% of rows, which empty_to_null turns into no claim.
address.fullOutlet Address["trim", "empty_to_null"]0.7Column 'Outlet Address' holds values like '10 Herberton St', '179 Nicklin Way' and 'Shop 124, Mount Pleasant Shopping Centre'. It is the street-level address of the outlet, so it is the physical address and not a postal or head-office address. It excludes suburb, state and postcode, which are in separate columns. A few values are not street addresses, e.g. 'A1 Exhausts & Mechanical Petrie', hence the lower confidence. Blank in about 11% of rows.
address.localityOutlet Suburb["trim", "empty_to_null"]0.92Column 'Outlet Suburb' holds values like 'MACKAY', 'TWEED HEADS SOUTH' and 'MAROOCHYDORE'. These are suburb or town names of the outlet, so address.locality. The rest of the address is in separate columns.
address.postcodeOutlet Postcode["trim", "empty_to_null", "postcode_normalize"]0.93Column 'Outlet Postcode' holds values like '4740', '2486' and '3216'. They are all 4-digit Australian postcodes of the outlet. postcode_normalize zero-pads any that lost a leading zero.

Outlet NameOutlet-level label such as 'Amplifon Mackay', 'ADPAQ' or 'Allcracft Sewing Machines' (a typo of the trading name). It is neither the registered legal name nor clearly a trading name, so it is not mapped.
Telephone NumberNo telephone field in the ontology.
Online Mail Order FlagScheme attribute (Yes/No) with no ontology field.
Australia Wide FlagScheme attribute (Yes/No) describing offer coverage, with no ontology field.
Overseas FlagScheme attribute (Yes/No) with no ontology field.
Senior Scheme FlagScheme-participation flag with no ontology field. It is 'Yes' on every profiled row.
Carer Scheme FlagScheme-participation flag with no ontology field.
Business CategoryDirectory-specific category such as 'Dentists' or 'Pharmacies', not an ANZSIC code, so it should not go in entity.industry_code.
DiscountFree-text description of the discount offer, with no ontology field. It is also truncated at about 60 characters in the profile.
LatitudeGeocoordinate with no ontology field. It holds 0 as a placeholder for unknown on some rows.
LongitudeGeocoordinate with no ontology field. It holds 0 as a placeholder for unknown on some rows.
