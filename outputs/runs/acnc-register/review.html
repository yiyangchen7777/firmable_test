映射审核 · acnc-register

PASSING WITH FLAGS 修订 1/3 · 流程步数 8 · 审核材料同步存于 review.md

怎么审（约 5 分钟）

  1. 看硬指标（校验结果）：有没有 ❌。校验位、枚举、唯一性是代码算的，不会说谎。
  2. 看语义疑点（语义抽检）：这是最需要人的部分——模型挑出「解析没错但含义可能错」的字段，你判断同不同意。
  3. 抽看对照样例（原始 vs 输出）：随手挑两条，问自己「左边这列的意思，真的是右边那个字段吗？」
  4. 扫一眼未映射清单（unmapped）：理由是否诚实，有没有本该映射却被偷懒放弃的列。

你只有三个动作：approve / reject + 意见 / abandon。你不能直接改配置文件——人一改，配置就成了「手写的」，作业的核心主张（agent 独立完成映射）就不成立了。

source_reliability
0.9 — ACNC is the national charity regulator and this is its own Charity Register published under CC-BY, so identity, name and registration claims are authoritative.
record_id
composite ['ABN', 'Charity_Legal_Name'] — ABN alone is not safe: it has about 2% blanks and some rows (e.g. 859701-Y9K2G5 PAF) have none. Charity_Legal_Name is 200 distinct over 200 rows with no nulls. The composite of ABN and Charity_Legal_Name is therefore unique per register row even when ABN is blank. The source has no explicit record key column.
observed_at
dataset_modified（basis: proxy，值 2026-10-11）— No per-record extract or last-updated column exists. Registration_Date and Date_Organisation_Established are historical facts (valid_from material), not assertion times. The dataset is refreshed weekly, so the catalogue modified date is used as a weak proxy.
