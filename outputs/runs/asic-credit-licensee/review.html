怎么审（约 5 分钟）

  1. 看硬指标（校验结果）：有没有 ❌。校验位、枚举、唯一性是代码算的，不会说谎。
  2. 看语义疑点（语义抽检）：这是最需要人的部分——模型挑出「解析没错但含义可能错」的字段，你判断同不同意。
  3. 抽看对照样例（原始 vs 输出）：随手挑两条，问自己「左边这列的意思，真的是右边那个字段吗？」
  4. 扫一眼未映射清单（unmapped）：理由是否诚实，有没有本该映射却被偷懒放弃的列。

你只有三个动作：approve / reject + 意见 / abandon。你不能直接改配置文件——人一改，配置就成了「手写的」，作业的核心主张（agent 独立完成映射）就不成立了。

未映射的列 · 11

源列理由
REGISTER_NAMEConstant 'Credit Licence' for every row (1 distinct value); names the register, not an entity attribute.
CRED_LIC_NUMLicence number: used as record_id (the source's own record key, 200 distinct over 200 rows). It is a record identifier, not an entity identifier, and has no ontology field.
CRED_LIC_START_DTdd/mm/yyyy licence start date (e.g. '04/11/2010'). It is when the licence was granted, not entity.date_registered (the company's registration date), and is not a record observation date; valid_from material only.
CRED_LIC_END_DT100% null in the profile; there is nothing to map.
CRED_LIC_AFSL_NUMAustralian Financial Services Licence number ('0' when none, otherwise often equal to the credit licence number). It is a licence identifier with no ontology field, not an ABN/ACN.
CRED_LIC_STATUS_HISTORY99.5% null free text about past suspensions (e.g. 'Suspended at the request of Licensee/Registrant 18/07/2018 1'); no ontology field.
CRED_LIC_LATGeocoded latitude; the ontology has no coordinate field.
CRED_LIC_LNGGeocoded longitude; the ontology has no coordinate field.
CRED_LIC_EDRSExternal dispute resolution scheme memberships (AFCA, COSL, FOS; '~'-separated); a relationship to other bodies with no ontology field.
CRED_LIC_AUTHORISATIONSFree-text description of licence authorisations; no ontology field.
CRED_LIC_STATUSValues such as 'APPR' describe the approval status of the credit licence, not the company's existence or registration status, so it does not map to entity.status. Unmapped per human reviewer.
